Our 0–100 Ranked score, calculated from public records: each place’s City of Chicago inspection record since January 2023 (40%, recent visits counting more), its years open at its address (30%) and hand-checked honors (30%), compared with other Chicago restaurants. Not an official City grade: the City reports Pass, Pass w/ Conditions or Fail. An inspection describes conditions on the day of the visit.

About once a week we rebuild the data from the City’s newest public records, and these pages with it. Cuisine groups are the app’s own: each place is filed under one, from its name, its City records and Overture Maps listings. Each place’s page in the web version links to its own City inspection records.
