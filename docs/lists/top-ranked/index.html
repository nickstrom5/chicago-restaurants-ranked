How it’s ranked

Our 0–100 Ranked score, not an official City grade. It blends each place's City inspection record (40%, recent visits counting more), how long it has been open at its address (30%; a chain store's years count half) and hand-checked honors (30%: Michelin, James Beard, Chicago icons and the like), compared with other Chicago restaurants. A part we don't have counts as average. 85 means it ranks above 85% of Chicago restaurants. A failed latest inspection holds a place to 50, and a failed inspection or pest citation in the past year to 80.
