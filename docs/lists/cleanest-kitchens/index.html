How it’s ranked

Our 0–100 inspection score, not an official City score. Based on City of Chicago inspections from January 2023 through Nov 1, 2026, with recent visits counting more: failed inspections, pest citations, serious food-safety violations, violations per visit, passes with conditions, and whether the last visit failed. Places need at least two inspections.

Not an official City grade: the City reports Pass, Pass w/ Conditions or Fail. An inspection describes conditions on the day of the visit.
