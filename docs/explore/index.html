Search Chicago restaurant grades

Look up nearly every restaurant, café and tavern the City of Chicago inspects, with our A–F grade calculated from City of Chicago food inspection records (not an official City grade), ranked lists like Cleanest Kitchens and Michelin & Beard, and a map. Plus restaurants across the rest of Illinois. The same data as the free iPhone and iPad app.
