Chicago restaurant grades by neighborhood

The web version of Chicago Restaurants: Ranked lists 7,786 places in 76 Chicago community areas (not counting airports, stadiums, cafeterias and other non-restaurants). 7,619 have our A–F grade, 3,193 of them an A. Pick a neighborhood to see its places, best inspection score first.

Our 0–100 score and A–F grade, calculated from City of Chicago inspection records since January 2023. Not official City grades: the City reports Pass, Pass w/ Conditions or Fail. An inspection describes conditions on the day of the visit.

City records through September 28, 2026. About once a week we rebuild the data from the City’s newest public records, and these pages with it. Source: City of Chicago Food Inspections on the Chicago Data Portal.
